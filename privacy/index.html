Privacy Policy

Last updated August 21, 2026

Sound Cue is designed to work without collecting personal data.

Data collection

Sound Cue does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not use accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Workout names, workout configurations, sound cues, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. Sound Cue does not transmit this information to the developer or to an external server.

You can delete saved workouts from within the app. Deleting Sound Cue removes its locally stored data from that device.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Sound Cue receives confirmation that an upgrade was purchased, but it does not receive or store your payment information. Apple’s handling of purchase information is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Support requests

If you open a support request, the information you choose to provide is used only to understand and respond to that request. Support requests in this repository are public, so do not include personal, payment, health, or other sensitive information.

Support is provided through GitHub, which processes information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

This website

This static support site does not include advertising or developer-operated analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical information when serving the site under its own privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If Sound Cue’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the new practices take effect. The date at the top of this policy identifies the latest revision.

Questions

For privacy questions, open a support request. Please do not include sensitive information.
